The three problems

  1. The parser reads dates month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so their dates come out flipped. This is the bug #4471 was opened for. Ticket: issue 1.
  2. The booking-v3 form tells day-first tenants to type MM/DD/YYYY. People do what the hint says, and the form reads it their way. The fix is the hint text, not the parser. Ticket: issue 2.
  3. Dates arrive one day off. The desk already marks these in desk-notes.json as a one-day move. The month is right, so this is not a flip: the texts point at time zones or DST (Lisbon and Toronto got different days for one meeting; "every booking in June"). Ticket: issue 3.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on subject alone, and most subject lines in the export cover more than one problem. Intake's tag is a guess from the reporter's browser: the "tagged as" column shows it often disagrees with the cause. A new decision record should replace 0001; an accepted decision is never edited.

Not sure

A few reports carry no dates in their text. They stay with the parser, the closest fit, until someone reads them again.

Lessons

  • Merge by cause, not by subject. Count each cause on its own.
  • The tenant (locale and booking form) tells the cause. The reporter's browser does not.
  • A "known" ticket with one fix queued is a claim. Check it against the reports.
